About the farm

Raised right, sold fairly

MAK Chickens and Hatcheries breeds and supplies quality roadrunners, guinea fowls, turkeys and broilers, alongside affordable fertile eggs and full hatchery services. Every bird leaving our farm is healthy, well cared for, and priced so small farmers and households can afford to grow their own flock.

Whether you're starting your first backyard coop or restocking a commercial run, we supply the birds, the eggs, and the hatching know-how to get you there.

Hatchery services

Bring us your eggs, we'll bring you chicks

Don't have an incubator? Our hatchery takes care of the whole process — from careful egg handling through to healthy, hatched chicks ready to collect.

  • Incubation of client-supplied fertile eggs
  • Temperature- and humidity-controlled hatching
  • Candling to check fertility partway through
  • Guidance on brooding once chicks hatch
